# ThriveHive

> ThriveHive was a Boston-based guided marketing platform for small businesses, founded in 2011 (Techstars Boston 2015). Acquired by Propel Marketing (GateHouse Media) in 2016 for $11.8M and folded into Gannett's LocaliQ brand after the 2019 GateHouse-Gannett merger. The company published no public API or developer portal; thrivehive.com now redirects to localiq.com behind an expired TLS certificate.
